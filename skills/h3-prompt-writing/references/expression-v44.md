## 1. 表现手段开关（SKILL.md 第 0 款整句，(v4.4-B) 起为补句）

**0. Expression switches (v4) — read first.** If the task text contains a section titled 「本项目表现手段开关」, read its four switches: Q版插入 (chibi inserts), 夸张特效 (exaggerated VFX), 网络流行语与谐音 (slang and censor-safe homophones), 段子与接梗 (jokes and comebacks). A switched-off device is not used anywhere in this chapter, and every rule below about it does not apply: write plain live-action acting instead, even if the storyboard still carries a 【表现】 line for it. If that section is absent, treat all four as on. A switch that is on only permits the device; the storyboard decides where. (v4.4-B) The switch text says a device is “not required in every chapter”: that means no piling on; it does not make zero the safe choice. When the storyboard hands you a 【表现】 line, write that device at full strength; never water it down into plain acting on your own. (2026-11-26) This does not apply to VFX: VFX are optional and stay in the shot's own framing by default (SKILL H2 item 4).

## 2. 卡姿兰大眼 S01 强版（替换 SKILL.md 第 4 款的 S01 行）

- **先选景别：默认原景别版（2026-11-26 待测，H2 第4条「特效可选、默认原景别」；主会话 seq2044）。** 分镜写了 S01，不等于要切双眼大特写。只有剧情本身就是「盯着他的眼睛看」时，才用下面的强版（切到双眼大特写）。其余一律不切镜，在本镜原来的景别里做：`<t0>–<t0+2>s: no cut, still in the <framing> of <Name>: his eyes suddenly fly wide open; his irises look visibly larger, clear, glossy and brightly lit as if a ring light had switched on in front of him, with two or three sharp white star-shaped catch-lights sparkling in each iris, clearly readable at this distance; <brows / hands reaction>, his lips stay closed. At <t0+2>s the catch-lights shrink to one small glint in each eye and his eyes return to normal size; <reaction action>.` 原景别版保留「变亮、变大、星形高光」三样，不写 extreme close-up / cuts to his eyes。
  - 正例（28#13 夜跑，中景不切镜，出片正常）：`… his eyes suddenly fly wide open: his irises look visibly larger, clear, glossy and brightly lit as if a ring light had switched on in front of him, with two or three sharp white star-shaped catch-lights sparkling in each iris, clearly readable at this distance; his brows lift and freeze, his lips stay closed.`
  - 反例：剧情只是他心里一惊，也照强版切到双眼大特写，镜头节奏被打断（28#13 返修前）。
- **强版（只在剧情就是看眼睛时用）：**

（2026-11-26）本强版只在剧情本身就是看眼睛、切特写明显更好时用；默认在本镜原有景别里做（H2 第 4 条）。

- Kakuzilan-style wide eyes (S01), allowed as a power-fantasy beat — strong version (v4.4-B). A plain `the eyes suddenly widen` renders as an ordinary startled stare (2026-11-16: two test rounds, both weak), so write it as light and optics, with one second of face first so the change can be seen: `<t0>–<t0+1>s: <framing> of <Name> in the <scene>, <concrete action>. At <t0+1>s, the camera cuts to an extreme close-up of both of <Name>'s eyes. Nobody speaks and no voice is heard in this shot. His eyelids fly open and the irises visibly swell to about one and a half times their size, clear, glossy and brightly lit as if a ring light had just switched on in front of him; three or four sharp white star-shaped catch-lights ignite inside each iris and a four-pointed glint flashes once at the outer corner of each eye; every eyelash stands out crisp and dark; a tiny mirror image of <trigger object> shines in both pupils; thin white speed lines flash around the edges of the frame for a few frames. His brows stay low and heavy and his jaw is set — the stunned, hungry stare of a grown man. The skin around the eyes is clean, smooth and evenly lit. At <t0+2.5>s, the camera cuts back to <framing>: the catch-lights have shrunk to one small glint in each eye, the eyes are back to normal size, <reaction action>; <Name> stays in frame until the last frame.` Keep all five visible parts (lit swelling irises, star catch-lights, corner glint, reflected object, speed lines); do not shorten it back to `eyes widen`. Do not add the word cute, girlish, kawaii or anime, not even to deny it.

## 3. G01 与 S01 的分工（v4.4-B 新增，位置在 SKILL.md 第 4 款「Pick the one effect…」之后）

**Which eye effect goes where (v4.4-B).** G01 golden eyes = value lands in his hands (an item turns into gold, money arrives). S01 wide eyes = someone cannot believe what he sees — the lead or a supporting character (e.g. the shop owner when the gold bar hits the counter). The storyboard chooses; write exactly the one it names. For a VFX shot marked `CUT@`, the two cuts of the S01 / G01 timeline (face → eyes → face) are the only cuts in the shot.

## 4. 不叠加与接力（SKILL.md 第 4 款整句，(v4.4-B) 起为补句）

Do not write heart-shaped eyes, star eyes, pink sparkles, a cute little train, flower-throwing, or stomping. Do not stack a chibi insert and a heavy VFX burst on the same second. (v4.4-B) They may sit in the same payoff sequence in different shots, with at least one live-action acting or dialogue shot between them; that is a relay, not stacking. Do not only write "warm gold light wells up" in a medium shot, and do not treat "he rubs his eye and looks again" as the effect.

## 5. 冷笑话、自嘲、冷面旁白（v4.4-B 新增，位置在 SKILL.md 第 11 款之后）

**11b. Deadpan jokes, self-mockery and dry asides (v4.4-B).** Lines marked `【表现】Ln｜段子…` or `【表现】Ln｜流行语…` are often inner-monologue lines in this show. Then every OS rule above still applies (C-001 of SKILL.md, r19: one fixed mouth shape that never forms words, a voice sentence with no name and no speech verb), and the joke is carried by timing and a cold face: the off-screen voice is dry, flat and unhurried, with a half-beat pause before the last words; on screen <Name> keeps a straight face, one eyebrow lifting a fraction or his eyes sliding sideways (rows 4, 7 or 9 of the mouth table), then he gets on with what he was doing. Fixed pattern (r19, 2026-11-24; the old `An off-screen … inner-monologue voice (S1) … narrates … Nobody on screen is speaking` pattern is void): mouth sentence once before the first time block `Inner-monologue shot for <Name>: his mouth is set in a still, flat line and never forms words; his voice is only an off-screen inner voice-over.`, and in the time block `(S1) inner voice, dry, flat, unhurried, with a half-beat pause before the last words, <d>[Chinese] <line, unchanged></d>` (no name, no `says` / `narrates`, nothing about the mouth after `</d>`). For a spoken comeback, <Name> does not look at the other person until the last word. He may say it with his back turned only if no other face is visible in the shot; if the other person's face is visible, he turns his head to at least a profile while he speaks, or the line goes into its own shot without the other face (v4.4-C, SKILL.md 「Faceless speaker vs a visible face」). No VFX, no chibi, no on-screen text, no laugh.

## 6. 正例：卡姿兰大眼（v4.4-B 新增）

**正例（卡姿兰大眼 · 加强写法，配角，v4.4-B）：** `__MINIMAX_H3_REF_1__ is Jiang Che, a young office worker. __MINIMAX_H3_REF_2__ is the gold shop owner. __MINIMAX_H3_REF_3__ is the gold-buying shop location. __MINIMAX_H3_REF_4__ is the gold bar. The video begins already inside the live-action gold-buying shop. The reference images are identity and location references only; never show a character sheet, turnaround, three-view, contact sheet, or any reference board in the frame, not even for a single frame at the start. Nobody speaks and no voice is heard in this shot. 0-1s: medium shot across the glass counter; the shop owner (__MINIMAX_H3_REF_2__) sits behind it on screen-right, lifting his head as Jiang Che's hand on screen-left sets the gold bar (__MINIMAX_H3_REF_4__) down on the counter with a heavy clack. At 1s, the camera cuts to an extreme close-up of both of the shop owner's eyes. His eyelids fly open and the irises visibly swell to about one and a half times their size, clear, glossy and brightly lit as if a ring light had just switched on in front of him; three or four sharp white star-shaped catch-lights ignite inside each iris and a four-pointed glint flashes once at the outer corner of each eye; every eyelash stands out crisp and dark; a tiny mirror image of the gold bar shines in both pupils; thin white speed lines flash around the edges of the frame for a few frames. His brows stay low and heavy and his jaw is set. The skin around the eyes is clean, smooth and evenly lit. At 2.5s, the camera cuts back to the medium shot across the counter: the catch-lights have shrunk to one small glint in each eye, his eyes are back to normal size, and he is already on his feet, both hands flat on the counter, leaning toward the gold bar with his lips closed; he stays in frame until the last frame. Sound: the clack of the bar, one short rising chime at the first cut, then the low hum of the shop.`

## 7. 正例：冷笑话心声（v4.4-B 新增）

**正例（冷笑话心声，v4.4-B）：** storyboard `dialogue` = `江澈（OS）：行，午饭这点委屈，我受得住！`, visual first line `【表现】L4｜段子｜原著自带` → (r19) before the first time block: `Inner-monologue shot for Jiang Che: his mouth is set in a still, flat line and never forms words; his voice is only an off-screen inner voice-over.` In the time block: `He weighs the gold bar once in his left hand, one eyebrow lifting a fraction, his eyes sliding sideways. The inner voice runs from 1s to 4s. (S1) inner voice, dry, flat, unhurried, with a half-beat pause before the last words, <d>[Chinese] 行，午饭这点委屈，我受得住！</d>`
