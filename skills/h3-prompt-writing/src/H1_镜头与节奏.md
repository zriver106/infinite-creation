## H1 镜头、运镜与打戏节奏

#### 〔H1-001〕 第2、3集通过后转正：**VFX stay in the current framing (TR-V05).** Ability effects (system glow, data in the eyes; chibi is a separate rule) play inside the shot's own framing; never cut to a close-up just for the effect. A shot that shows a system panel always keeps a fixed camera (manual M51 item 2, M62).

#### 〔H1-002〕 第2、3集通过后转正：**Fight rhythm at normal speed (TR-V08).** Cause and effect follow each other with no idle gap; hit to fall within 1 second; the result (lost tooth, blood, kept within platform review limits, no gory close-ups) appears as he lands and only then does anyone speak; bystanders react in the same second; a heavy object drops as soon as it is in the hand; a crowd stepping back walks backwards with feet on the ground and never glides. Write `normal speed, no slow motion, nobody freezes.`

### 〔H1-003〕 Camera, one-take and fight shots — 机位运镜与一镜到底

Applies to every shot's `video_prompt`. Read the shot's `camera` field first: the storyboard stage marks it `FIXED`, `MOVE`, `ONE-TAKE` or `CUT@Ns` (see sd-storyboard-engine「机位运镜与一镜到底」). If the field has no mark, treat it as `FIXED` unless the storyboard text clearly describes one continuous camera path. `FIXED` fixes the camera only; people keep breathing and micro-actions, and a scene that is in motion stays in motion (H1-005). The inner-monologue rules, the Q-version / VFX rules, the opening "video begins already inside…" sentence and the expression switches all stay in force; this section never overrides them (an OS block stays a frontal close-up, steady or slow push-in; the 1–2 s of a Q-version insert keeps the camera still).

**1. One camera instruction ≈ one cut.** H3 reads every new camera verb (`pushes in`, `pans`, `whips`, `cuts to`, `orbits`) as a new shot. So:
- `FIXED`: one sentence, once: `The camera holds a fixed position (static shot) for the whole shot.` Time blocks after that describe only action and sound.
- `MOVE`: one small, slow move written once; no second camera verb. Sentence form (v4.4): item 1 of `fight-v44.md`, loaded with skill_reference(name="h3-prompt-writing", ref="fight-v44.md").
- `ONE-TAKE`: one continuous move, stated once at the start, with at most two continuation sentences at path nodes, each phrased as the same move continuing.
- `CUT@Ns`: write the cut only at the marked second: `At <N>s, the camera cuts to <new framing, side, distance>.` No other camera verbs in between.

**2. One-take template (`ONE-TAKE`, 8–15 s).** Fill the 〔…〕 slots; keep everything else.
> `This is one continuous take with no cuts from the first frame to the last. 0s: 〔framing〕, the camera starts 〔behind / beside / in front of〕 〔who〕, about 〔N〕 metres away at 〔chest / eye / knee〕 height; 〔who〕 is on screen-〔left/right/centre〕 facing 〔direction〕, 〔who〕 is on screen-〔…〕 facing 〔…〕; 〔prop〕 is in 〔whose〕 〔left/right〕 hand. 0-〔t1〕s: 〔action〕; the camera 〔follows / tracks alongside / pushes forward〕 at 〔slow / steady / walking〕 speed. 〔t1〕-〔t2〕s: 〔action〕; the same unbroken move carries the camera past 〔landmark〕 〔toward / around to the right side of〕 〔who〕. 〔t2〕-〔end〕s: the camera slows and settles into 〔end framing〕 of 〔who〕, 〔positions and facings at the end〕; 〔held micro-action or dialogue〕. The camera never passes through walls, glass, furniture or people.`
- Every time block still needs a visible body action (a moving camera does not count as action). The camera may keep drifting while a character freezes for a beat — that contrast reads well; do not make both camera and character violent at once.
- Never write `cuts to`, `whip pan`, `quick cuts`, `dynamic camera`, `shaky cam throughout`, or a new camera verb in every block inside a one-take shot.
- If the path crosses the line between two characters, write the visible route (`the camera circles behind A's back to his right side`); never jump sides.

**3. Fight shots — v4.4 必读（硬规矩）.** 写打斗/冲击镜前必须先调用 skill_reference(name="h3-prompt-writing", ref="fight-v44.md") 读全文。Hard rule: before writing any impact or reaction shot, or a walking / closing-in / chasing shot, call that tool and read the whole file. All fight wording now lives only there: impact shot, reaction shot, force and injury, the four things per exchange, attack axis, taunts, no VFX, gait, examples and counter-examples.

**4. Space-time whiteboard (时空白板).** When the storyboard gives a `时空白板` line, carry each time point into the matching time block in English: camera position, every character's screen side and facing, and who holds each prop. The last time point is the end frame; describe it exactly so the next shot can start from it.

**正例与反例（ONE-TAKE 逼近、冲击镜、打斗反例）：** 见 `fight-v44.md` 第 2、4、5 条。

**5. Camera moves and cut craft — 运镜与镜头手法（按需读取）.** Before writing a shot whose `camera` is not `FIXED`, a shot with `CUT@` or more than one `[Shot N]`, or a shot the storyboard marks with a named move (low-angle entrance, arc, POV, dolly zoom, occlusion point, handheld, low tracking), call skill(name="h3-camera-moves") and follow its CAM items. It is read on its own and does not count toward the 40,000-character volume budget.

#### 〔H1-004〕 【待测】写明机位在哪个空间；人和物是实心的，不穿过栏杆、墙、家具（G23，txt L141、L151）

- 原则：提示词不说机位在里面还是外面，H3 会照场景参考图的视角拍，戏在里面却拍成外面。不写路线，H3 会让人从栏杆、墙、家具里穿过去。H1-003 第 2 条已写机位不穿墙，本条管机位所在的空间和人的路线；机位在哪、路线怎么走由分镜定（adapt-rules S-018），本条只管落到提示词。
- 适用条件：戏在车内、电梯、隔间这类有里外之分的空间的镜；人物要走动、过门、上下楼或经过栏杆、柜台、家具的镜；写视频提示词的人。
- 正确写法：
  1. 开场句照分镜写明机位在哪个空间、对着谁，并写一句全镜不离开这个空间。机位句只写一次，仍按 H1-003 的镜头形态。
  2. 绑定的场景图是外观视角而这一镜在里面：场景图只当里面看得见的那部分背景用（J-002 的「background only」写法），并写明画面不出现外观；没有可用的内景图，在报告里写明，退回补资产，不硬写。
  3. 人物走动写出看得见的路线：从哪一侧到哪一侧、在障碍物的哪一边，并加一句实心句。
  4. 分镜没有让人上下楼、过门，就不写 stairs、climbs、walks through 这类词；场景里有楼梯、栏杆而这一镜不用，写明它只在背景里、没有人靠近。
  5. 分镜要求上下楼或过门：写清脚踩在台阶上、手扶扶手、栏杆在身体外侧；过门写开门、走过、门在身后三步。
  6. 分镜没写机位在哪个空间，或写的路线要穿过实体才走得通：不自己猜，在报告里写明，退回分镜（S-018）。
- 可用短句：
  - `The camera is inside the car, on the front passenger side, facing <Name> in the driver's seat; the whole shot stays inside the car and never shows the car from outside.`
  - `People and objects are solid: nobody passes through railings, walls, glass, doors, counters, furniture, car bodies or other people.`
  - `<Name> walks along the open floor on the near side of the railing, from screen-left to screen-right; the railing stays between him and the stairwell the whole time.`
  - `The staircase stays in the background; nobody steps onto it or leans on its railing in this shot.`
  - `<Name> climbs three steps with each foot landing on a step, one hand on the handrail; the railing stays on the outer side of his body.`
  - 报告句：`Shot <n>: the storyboard does not say which space the camera is in / the route crosses a solid object; returned to storyboard.`

#### 〔H1-005〕 【待测】【分镜】【提示词】行进中的戏在行进中拍，静态对话不硬加运动（2026-11-26 用户点出）

- 目的：用户原话：「画面能动的就别停下来，镜头只有动起来才有质感，不然和看小说有啥区别」「但是也别硬塞，有些镜头明明是静态对话的，就别去硬加运动」。动不动由剧情决定。
- 做法：
  1. 剧情本身在行进中的戏（走路、跑步、开车、做事，边走边说、边跑边想、边做事边聊），照分镜在行进中写，不改成停下来站着拍；分镜 `camera` 标了 `MOVE` / `ONE-TAKE` 的，按第 1、2 条写一个推、拉、跟、摇、移的运动。行进中的台词照 E-003 让嘴可见；行进中的心声照 C-001 第 7 条保持正面或近正面近景，镜头在他前方后退跟拍、取景相对他的脸保持稳定。
  2. 本来就是坐着、站着对话的戏，按静态对话拍：靠表情、视线、小动作和正反打撑住，不硬加走位或运镜。
  3. 静止镜（停顿、凝视、对峙、静态对话）也写呼吸和微动作（眨眼、手指收紧、肩膀起伏），人不僵住。
  4. 标 `FIXED` 的镜，提示词不自己加镜头动词（一个镜头动词≈一次切镜，第 1 条）。
- 要求：有系统光幕的镜仍固定机位（H1-001）；没台词的行进时段只写脚步、风声等非人声（E-013）；对某人某物做反应时视线朝向对象（FX-013）。不新增出片拦截。
- 校验：交稿前看每个时段：分镜写了行进的，提示词里是否还在行进？静态对话镜有没有被加上走位或运镜？每个时段有没有可见的微动作？
- 正例（原著就是跑步路过时；原著是散步就照散步写，A-007）：他边跑边路过公园，扭头看向空地上打太极的老人（这一段没人说话，只有脚步和风声），再转成他边跑边看着老人方向的正面中近景，心声在跑动中出现。两人坐在桌边谈事，固定机位正反打，靠眼神和手上的茶杯撑住。
- 反例：人物停在原地正对镜头念心声，他评论的老人在他身后的背景里；或两人坐着说话，硬加环绕运镜、让一人无故起身走动。
