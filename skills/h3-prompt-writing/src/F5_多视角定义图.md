## F5 多人同场多视角定义图（_MV）

### 〔F5V-001〕 Multi-view scene sheet (`_MV`) for shots with two or more people — 多人同场绑多视角定义图

Source: moviePro `docs/临时规则/场景多视角定义图_临时规则.md`.

- **When:** a shot has two or more people in the same location, and that location has a `<场景号>_MV` asset (one 16:9 2×2 sheet: A main view, B reverse view, C side view, D 90-degree top-down floor plan). Single-person shots keep the ordinary single scene image.
- **Binding:** bind the `_MV` asset in `scene_ids` *instead of* the single scene image. It takes one reference slot. Never bind both for the same location.
- **No `_MV` yet for this location:** the managing session first asks am for the sheet and holds this location's multi-person shots until it arrives (about one hour); only after that may they be written without it. If you are given such a shot, keep the ordinary single scene image, write the shot normally (no spatial paragraph), and say in your report `Location <name> has no _MV sheet; multi-person blocking is unanchored.` Never ask IC to generate a sheet. A sheet may be named `<场景号>_MV·…` or `…·多视角定义图`; both count as the `_MV` sheet. (2026-11-26, Andy「我们之前做了场景多视角图 没使用吗」)
- **Position table (`scene_blocking` in the brief):** the location's D-plan position table is stored in the `_MV` asset's description and handed to you as `scene_blocking` (where each person stands or sits on D, which side the door, window, aisle and fixed furniture are on, which of A/B/C each multi-person shot is shot from). Follow it in every shot of that location, in every chapter, single-person shots included; if it conflicts with `visual`, the table wins. Only a scripted move changes a position, and the move is written in the shot. Empty `scene_blocking` = no table yet: say so in your report. (2026-11-26, Andy「一致性 变来变去」)
- **The prompt must contain this spatial paragraph as the sheet's REF definition line** (replace N with the sheet's REF number). Keep it one sentence (colon and commas, no full stops or semicolons inside), so the lint still reads the headcount sentence as the first content sentence:
  `__MINIMAX_H3_REF_N__ is a multi-view spatial definition sheet of one single location, not a storyboard and not the first frame of the video: panel A is the main view of the location, panel B the reverse view of the same location, panel C a side view of the same location and panel D a 90-degree orthographic top-down floor plan, all four panels show the same physical space, the final video shows only one normal perspective shot and never shows the grid, the panel letters or the floor plan, object positions and spatial directions come from D while appearance, materials and structure come from A, B and C, and when the camera moves the doors, windows, furniture, walls and the characters' positions keep the same relative layout.`
- **Per person:** say where each person is on the D plan (e.g. "at the window-side workstation", "in the aisle behind the desks"), and say which direction the shot is taken from ("shot from the A direction", "from the C direction, along the aisle"). These go alongside the headcount first sentence; they do not replace it. Name what is behind them in that direction, taken from the panel (e.g. "shot from the B direction, the reception counter and the two potted trees behind them"); screen-left and screen-right follow from that direction and the table, never chosen freely shot by shot.
- **Never** describe the sheet's layout as the picture ("a four-panel image", "split screen"), never ask for panel letters or a floor plan on screen, and do not write compass directions.
- The `_MV` sheet carries no story props or people; hand-held or changing objects still come from prop references and the prompt.

### 〔F5V-002〕 【待测】【分镜】【提示词】同场在场的人照 `scene_cast` 留在画面里，站位要合理（2026-11-26 用户点出）

- `scene_cast.present` 里在场、按站位表落在本镜画框内的人，写成背景里不说话的路人（在哪、做什么、穿什么）；人数锁只数有名字、绑了参考的人。说话双方面对或侧对，不背对说话对象；人不站进家具、墙、床里。反例：EP21 大堂十来个同学在场，每镜只写两人，大堂空着，第 21 镜人群又突然出现。
