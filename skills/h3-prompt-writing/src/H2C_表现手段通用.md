## H2C 表现手段通用（开关、决定来源、v4.4 必读）

### 〔H2C-001〕 Expression switches, where the decisions come from, v4.4 rule — moved verbatim from H2-001, 2026-11-25

**v4.4 必读（硬规矩）.** 写 Q版/金光/卡姿兰/接力前必须先调用 skill_reference(name="h3-prompt-writing", ref="expression-v44.md") 读全文。Hard rule: before writing any shot or line that has a 【表现】 line (chibi, G01, S01, a chibi / VFX relay, slang, jokes), call that tool and read the whole file; it is the v4.4 text and wins over this section where they differ. Exception (2026-11-26): for VFX, H2 item 4 wins over `expression-v44.md` (optional, own framing, no forced close-up or full strength).

**0. Expression switches (v4) — read first.** If the task text contains a section titled 「本项目表现手段开关」, read its four switches: Q版插入 (chibi inserts), 夸张特效 (exaggerated VFX), 网络流行语与谐音 (slang and censor-safe homophones), 段子与接梗 (jokes and comebacks). A switched-off device is not used anywhere in this chapter, and every rule below about it does not apply: write plain live-action acting instead, even if the storyboard still carries a 【表现】 line for it. If that section is absent, treat all four as on. A switch that is on only permits the device; the storyboard decides where.

**0b. Where the decisions come from.** The storyboard marks every chosen device with a first line in `visual` / `sub_shots` such as `【表现】P3｜特效·G01 金光眼｜0.0–1.8s｜…`, `【表现】P4｜Q版·方式一（身旁小人）｜1.0–3.0s｜…`, `【表现】P6｜Q版·方式二（换头）｜…`, or `【表现】L1｜谐音｜配音替换：「我cy！」读作「我去！」`. Turn each 【表现】 line into the English visible state described below, at the time window it gives. For VFX this is a suggestion only (H2 item 4). Never copy the 【表现】 line, the Pn/Ln codes or any Chinese bookkeeping into the prompt. If a shot has no 【表现】 line, do not add a chibi or a VFX burst on your own.
