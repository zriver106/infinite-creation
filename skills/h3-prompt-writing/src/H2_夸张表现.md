## H2 Q 版、特效、流行语与谐音（冷门）

### 〔H2-001〕 Q-version (chibi), exaggerated VFX, slang and homophones — hard rule

The show's baseline is photorealistic live-action. A Q-version insert is a short, extreme-emotion exception, not a style change for the whole shot. Two presentation modes follow the 2026-11-11 definition: a chibi miniature beside the character, or a brief chibi head swap. The 2026-10-02 ban on head-swaps is revoked. A brief head swap is allowed; the live-action face must return within 1–3 seconds.

**Slang, jokes and homophones (items 10, 11 and the 谐音 example) moved verbatim to volume H2L on 2026-11-25; read H2L when the shot has a slang / joke / homophone 【表现】 line.**

**Items 0 and 0b and the v4.4 rule moved verbatim to volume H2C (2026-11-25); H2C is read with every 【表现】 shot.**

**1. When it appears.** Only if the shot has a `【表现】…Q版·方式一` or `【表现】…Q版·方式二` line. The storyboard places these only on an extreme-emotion beat (cognitive shock, sudden windfall, collapse, or extreme joy), one mode per beat, at most two per episode. Ordinary surprise, a normal smile, ordinary anger, and serious or sad beats (a breakup, betrayal, grief) get real acting only — no chibi, no cute effects.

**2. Mode 1 — chibi miniature beside the character (positive wording).** Write a short visible action, then a return. The chibi's motion must be exaggerated and unrestrained — a sliding kneel, clutching the belly laughing, clutching the head with both hands, or both fists pumped in the air. Never a meek standing pose, a cute idle, a slight nod, or a kawaii wave.

> For about 1–2 seconds, a small super-deformed chibi miniature of <Name> pops in beside <Name>'s head, about as tall as his real head, matching the hair colour and outfit colours of __MINIMAX_H3_REF_N__, with a cool deadpan face, and [one exaggerated unrestrained action: a sliding kneel / clutching the belly laughing / clutching the head with both hands / both fists pumped in the air]. The live-action <Name> and the real scene stay fully visible and photorealistic; the chibi never covers his eyes, mouth, the key prop, or the system panel. Then the chibi disappears, and the shot stays live-action.

**3. Mode 2 — chibi head swap (positive wording).** Write the swap and the revert in the same shot:

> For about 1–2 seconds, <Name>'s head briefly becomes a super-deformed chibi head matching the hair colour of __MINIMAX_H3_REF_N__, with [specific expression, e.g. a dead-fish-eye look of shock, the head cocked hard to one side], while his live-action body, clothes and the scene stay photorealistic. Then his head returns to the live-action face of __MINIMAX_H3_REF_N__ and stays that way for the rest of the shot.

**3b. Chibi reference asset (hard rule, v4.2).** Every chibi shot must have the character's chibi design asset 「<Name>·Q版设定」 bound in the shot's props, so it arrives as its own reference image (label like `主角·Q版设定（道具）`) next to the live-action character image. In `subject_definitions` write it as: `__MINIMAX_H3_REF_N__ is the chibi design of <Name>, used only as the style and identity reference for the brief chibi miniature / chibi head; this design image itself never appears in the frame.` In Mode 1 / Mode 2 sentences, match the chibi to that REF (`a small super-deformed chibi miniature of <Name> matching __MINIMAX_H3_REF_N__ (the chibi design)`), while the live-action body and face still follow the live-action character REF. Never treat the chibi design as a prop on screen. If a shot carries a 【表现】 Q版 line but its references contain no chibi design image, write no chibi: use plain live-action acting and say so in your report, so the binding can be fixed.

**4. VFX — optional, in the shot's own framing (2026-11-26; user: 特效不必切近景，规则不是强制性的).** Item 4 is advice, not a hard rule; the chibi items are unchanged.
- 目的：特效是加分手段，不是固定格式。硬切眼部大特写或单拆特效镜，效果不好，前后镜也接不上（同 H1-001）。
- 做法：①【表现】特效行表示这一拍可以用，不等于必须单做特效镜或切近景。默认在本镜原有景别（中景、中近景）里用表情加轻量效果，人物和场景照常入画。②只有剧情就是看眼睛变化、近景明显更好时，才推近或切短特写，再回本镜景别（S01 强版即此情形）。③建议只用【表现】行点名的一个效果，不叠加。④建议特效窗口不放 `<d>`，台词、心声放别的时段或相邻镜；心声镜的原有景别就是 C-001 第 7 条的正面近景。⑤写成看得见的状态（虹膜变色、光点），不只一句暖光，不把「揉眼再看」当特效。
- 要求：不新增出片拦截；分镜无【表现】行不自加；开关关闭不用（H2C 第 0 条）。
- 校验：切了近景/特写的，报告写一句理由。

Preferred positive sentences (`<framing>` = this shot's existing framing):

- Golden eyes (G01): `In the same <framing>, for about 1–2 seconds, nobody speaks: <Name>'s irises turn bright glowing gold, thin golden rays and gold particles shoot outward, a brief gold flare rims the eyelids; then the glow settles to a residual gold tint.`
- Kakuzilan-style wide eyes (S01): the strong version (`expression-v44.md` item 2) only in case 4②; otherwise widen the eyes in this shot's framing.
- Pupils snap wide (S02): `In the same <framing>, for about one second, <Name>'s pupils suddenly contract to pinpoints and then snap wide, the eyes opening fully, a sharp catch-light flashing in each eye; his whole body freezes for a beat.`
- Time freeze (S07): `For about one second the moment freezes: <Name> and everything around him hold perfectly still mid-motion, the colours briefly desaturate except for <Name> and <key object>, then motion and colour snap back.`
- Golden treasure glint (G07): `As <Name> looks down at <object>, a sharp four-pointed golden star glint flares on its edge for half a second and a faint warm glow rises from it, lighting his face from below.`
- Throbbing temple vein (A01): `In the same <framing>, a vein on <Name>'s temple visibly throbs twice, his jaw clenches, his eyes narrow and stay on <target>; the rest of his face stays still.`
- Awakening eye glint (P01): `In the same <framing>, a cold bright glint sweeps across both of <Name>'s irises from left to right, his gaze sharpening and locking forward.`
- Tooth gleam (W02, only after a payoff): `<Name> gives a slow one-sided smirk toward the camera; a single bright star-shaped gleam flashes on his front tooth for a fraction of a second.`
- Dead-fish eyes, live-action (E04): `<Name>'s eyelids drop to half-closed, his eyes go flat and lifeless, his face completely deadpan for about a second.`

Do not write heart-shaped eyes, star eyes, pink sparkles, a cute little train, flower-throwing, or stomping. Do not stack a chibi insert and a heavy VFX burst on the same second.

正例：中近景里他看着金条，同一景别中虹膜转金、光点外射，随后收住、嘴角压不住笑，下一镜直接接上。
反例：中景对话中间硬切 1–2 秒满屏眼睛特写，前后镜接不上；或一有【表现】行就单拆 2 秒无声特效镜。

**4b. No empty frames after the effect (advice).** H3 fills leftover time with an empty corridor if the timeline ends early. After the effect window keep <Name> in the same framing and scene with a concrete reaction (he looks down at the gold bar, the corners of his mouth fighting a grin) until the last frame; after a close-up (4②) return to the shot's framing. Never end the timeline before the shot ends.

**4c. Prefer no stand-alone 2–3 s silent VFX or chibi shots** (they opened on the character sheet, then drifted to an empty corridor). Prefer the effect inside an existing shot (4①). A separate VFX or chibi shot gets at least 4 seconds: at least 1 s of in-scene live-action acting, the effect or chibi window, then live-action to the last frame; a 2–3 s one from the storyboard still gets that full timeline.

**5. Style lock.** Never turn the whole shot into anime, cel-shading, or a full-screen chibi scene. The corridor, street, body, costume and skin stay photorealistic before, during (except the brief chibi head in Mode 2), and after the insert. If a chibi reference image is attached, bind it as the chibi only; the live-action character still follows the live-action reference.

**6. Mouth and OS.** If the beat is an OS / inner-monologue shot, the 心声 rules still apply (C-001 rule 3 shot declaration and `Mouth shape:` phrases, the rule 8 `… narrates <d>…</d>. Nobody on screen is speaking; …` voice sentence, no rule-4 banned words; Inner-Monologue section). The chibi may make an exaggerated expression, but it must not cover the live-action mouth. If the beat is spoken dialogue (e.g. `我去！`), the live-action mouth may speak that line. In Mode 2 nobody speaks and no OS line plays during the 1–3 second chibi-head window; put the line before the swap or after the live-action face has returned.

**7. No ledger words.** Do not write Chinese bookkeeping such as `【表现】`, `P3`, `L1`, `配音替换`, `Q版方式一`, `换头`, `特效G01` in the prompt. Those decisions stay in the storyboard; the prompt only describes the visible state in English.

**正例（方式二，冷却打击）：** `The video begins already inside the live-action street outside the gold shop. The reference images are identity and location references only; never show a character sheet, turnaround, three-view, contact sheet, or any reference board in the frame, not even for a single frame at the start. 0s: frontal medium shot, the lead already standing centre frame on the pavement facing the camera, his right hand sliding off his forehead. A translucent cyan-blue holographic panel with a thin gold border floats in mid-air in the empty space at the left side of the frame, beside his shoulder and head, a full arm's length away from his body; his chest and shirt stay fully visible. For about two seconds, the lead's head briefly becomes a super-deformed chibi head matching the hair colour of __MINIMAX_H3_REF_1__, with a dead-fish-eye look of shock, the head cocked hard to one side, while his live-action body and the street stay photorealistic. Then his head returns to the live-action face of __MINIMAX_H3_REF_1__ and stays that way; the panel dims and fades away.`

**反例：** `cute chibi, sparkles, oversized kawaii eyes, anime style throughout, he turns into a cartoon character and remains that way`；或只写 `add Q version` 而不写位置、时长和回到本相；或开场不写场景内起幅，让人物三视图/设定图出现在画面里；或把系统光幕写成 `floats in front of his chest` 贴在身上；或把金光眼只写成一句模糊的暖光、把「揉眼再看」当成特效一带而过（在中景里做特效本身没有问题，见第 4 条）。

#### 〔H2-002〕 第2、3集通过后转正：**Chibi shots last at least 4 seconds and carry an effect (TR-V07).** This item applies only when the chibi rules of this skill already allow a chibi shot; chibi is reserved for extreme emotion and used sparingly, and this item does not encourage more chibi shots.
