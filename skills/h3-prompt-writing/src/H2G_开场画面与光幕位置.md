## H2G 开场画面与光幕位置（每镜必读）

### 〔H2G-001〕 Opening frame and system panel position — hard rules (every shot)

**No device unless marked.** Without a 【表现】 line, write plain live-action acting and add no chibi, effect or slang on your own; never copy 【表现】 lines or Pn/Ln codes into the prompt.

**8. The shot must open already inside the scene (hard rule). Reference images, character sheets and three-views must never appear in the frame.** In this project a character reference is often a character sheet / three-view (left: a face close-up; right: front, side and back full-body stands on a light or white background). That image is an identity reference only. Never show a character sheet, turnaround, three-view, contact sheet, style board, or any reference board in the video, not even for a single frame at the start. Do not write "character sheet", "turnaround", "three-view", "reference board", or "white-backdrop lineup" as visible content. Even if the storyboard says "continuing from the previous shot", this shot often has no first-frame image attached — you must still write a concrete in-scene opening at 0s (place, camera, body position, lighting) so the first frame is already the corridor, the street, or the room, never the sheet. Put this lock near the start of every Ref2VA prompt:

> The video begins already inside the live-action <scene>. The reference images are identity and location references only; never show a character sheet, turnaround, three-view, contact sheet, or any reference board in the frame, not even for a single frame at the start. 0s: <concrete in-scene composition>.

**9. System panel position (hard rule).** If the storyboard requires the system holographic panel, write it as a translucent cyan-blue holographic panel with a thin gold border (a plain, even translucent colour; no banners, colour bars, row frames or grid lines inside it), floating in mid-air off to one side of the character at shoulder-to-head height, in the empty part of the frame beside him, so that his chest and shirt stay fully visible; separated from the body by a clear gap of air. Frame the character off-centre so the panel has its own space — never stuck to the chest, never worn like a badge, never fused to the shirt. It appears only when the storyboard says it is on. If the previous beat already faded it out, do not write "still floats in front of his chest". A blue lanyard ID badge on the costume is clothing, not the panel — do not light the badge up as a UI.
Allowed wording: "off to one side, a full arm's length away" or "in the empty space at the left / right side of the frame, beside the shoulder and head". A large panel facing the camera is allowed if the face, chest and shirt stay fully visible; do not treat "filling about two-thirds of the frame" as a required fixed width. In a frontal shot, do not write the panel "in front of him" or "directly in front". What the panel says follows TR-V04 (〔D-001〕 in section D): at most two short lines, or no readable text.
